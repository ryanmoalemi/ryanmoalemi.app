Unspin

Privacy Policy - Unspin

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Unspin is a free educational coach for commonly taught canalith repositioning steps (the Epley sequence). It is not a doctor, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose BPPV or any other condition.

What Unspin stores

Unspin does not collect personal data on any server. There is no account, no analytics suite, no advertising SDK, and no cloud sync.

On this device only, the app may store:

  • Session log: date, ear side you chose, completed steps, optional better / same / worse self-report, whether motion sensors were live, and hold duration
  • Settings (voice, haptics, motion assist, hold time, appearance)
  • The one-time safety-disclaimer acknowledgment

Motion sensors

When Motion assist is on, Unspin uses the phone's orientation sensors live to coach head angles. Raw IMU streams are not uploaded and are not written to the session log.

Clearing data

Settings → Clear all local data removes the session log, settings, and disclaimer acknowledgment on this phone.

Children

Unspin is not directed at children. It does not collect contact information or create accounts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the hosted page will be updated.

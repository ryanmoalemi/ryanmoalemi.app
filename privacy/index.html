Unspin

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Unspin is a free app by Ryan Moalemi. It helps you run guided head-positioning sessions (such as Epley-style maneuvers) using on-device coaching. This policy explains what information Unspin handles and what it does not.

Summary

  • Unspin is free.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • Session history stays on your device.
  • Motion and orientation sensors are used on the device only and are not uploaded.

Information Unspin uses on your device

Unspin may process the following information locally on your phone or tablet so the app can work:

  • Motion and orientation sensors (such as gyroscope and accelerometer) to show head-alignment feedback during a guided session. This sensor data is processed on the device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Session log you create in the app (for example date, which side you practiced, steps completed, and a simple self-report like better / same / worse). This log is stored on your device only.
  • Settings you choose in the app (for example haptics or voice prompts). These stay on your device.

What we do not collect

  • No user accounts, profiles, or passwords.
  • No cloud sync of session logs in the current version.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • No advertising ID based tracking for ads in Unspin.

If a future version adds optional online features, this policy will be updated before those features collect anything new.

Permissions

Unspin may request access to motion and fitness or similar device sensors so alignment coaching can work. You can deny sensor access; the app can still offer timer-based guidance without sensor overlay. You can clear local session data from the app settings (or by uninstalling the app).

Children

Unspin is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Unspin is not medical care and is not a substitute for a doctor or physical therapist. Seek professional care for red-flag symptoms (such as sudden hearing loss, weakness, numbness, severe headache, or trouble speaking). Use the app at your own risk.

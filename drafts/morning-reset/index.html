A quieter morning reset

Before the laptop opens, Ryan spends about ten minutes on the same short reset. Water first, then a window, then one page of paper.

  • Drink a glass of water before looking at a screen.
  • Stand at a window long enough to notice the light.
  • Write one sentence about the first thing that needs doing.

The point is to start the day with one sentence, not with an inbox.

This page is a sample draft for the article editor. The ten-minute length is a personal guess, not a measured result.

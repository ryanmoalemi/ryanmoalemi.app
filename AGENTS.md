# Agent notes

## Section order

Put the most engaging content right under the intro or hero. Lead with app cards, screenshots, photos, and tools. Then order the rest from most interesting to least interesting. End with notes, disclosures, and fine print.

Only move existing sections. Do not rewrite or remove copy. Keep the full hero title above the fold on desktop and mobile. Keep each photo's credit with the photo. Do not move or change privacy policy pages. If a page is already in this order, leave it alone.

## Publishing

No em dashes.

Fact-check titles, meta descriptions, alt text, and structured data on AI-assisted content before publishing.
